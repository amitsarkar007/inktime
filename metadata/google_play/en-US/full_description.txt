InkTime is a calm, e-ink inspired dashboard for clocks, weather and world time.

Designed for readability, low visual noise and monochrome devices, InkTime combines a large 24-hour clock with GPS-based metric weather, sunrise and sunset details, air quality, pollen, pressure, UV index, and a practical forecast view.

Features:
- Large 24-hour time and date display
- One-shot GPS weather lookup
- Open-Meteo weather data with no API keys
- Current temperature, feels-like temperature, humidity, wind, pressure and precipitation
- UV index, air quality, PM2.5, PM10 and pollen where available
- Sunrise, sunset and daylight duration
- Rest-of-day hourly forecast
- Next 5 day forecast
- Saved world clocks with city search and persistent storage
- Monochrome light and dark modes
- Metric/SI units only

InkTime uses Open-Meteo APIs and does not require a paid weather API.
